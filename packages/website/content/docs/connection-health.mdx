title: Connection health
description: Green, blue, red — and how to reconnect.
---

Butin is honest about whether a session still works. Every service is in one of three states, shown by its
sidebar dot and a status pill:

- 🟢 **Green — verified.** Confirmed working _this session_ — a successful fetch, a Test, or a Reconnect
  proved the session is live.
- 🔵 **Blue — stored.** A session is saved and survived restart, but hasn't been checked yet this
  session. Usually fine — run **Test** to confirm.
- 🔴 **Red — expired.** The session is gone, or a fetch came back "logged out." **Reconnect** to sign in
  again. Your already-downloaded data is not affected.

For key-based services (like AWS), "connected" means the configuration is filled in rather than a live
session.

## Managing a connection

Each service gathers its controls in one place:

- **Reconnect** — re-run the login (or edit the config form, for key-based services).
- **Test** — probe the session right now.
- **Disconnect** — drop the stored session. Your downloaded files are kept.
- **Enable / disable** — hide a service from the sidebar and Overview without deleting its data.

> Blue just means "saved but not checked this session." It does **not** mean something's wrong — a quick
> Test turns it green.
