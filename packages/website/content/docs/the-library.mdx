---
title: Your data & exports
description: Where your data lives, full exports, and erasing it.
---

Everything Butin pulls lives in one place: **`~/butin`** in your home folder.

```text
~/butin/
  config.json          ← your encrypted sessions and per-service settings
  <service>/
    reports/           ← the data behind each tab (billing, usage, …)
    documents/         ← downloaded files (invoices, PDFs, records)
```

Sessions are **encrypted** (via your OS keychain). Reports and downloaded files sit in plain folders you
can open and browse.

## Pulling everything

- **Save everything** (on a service's settings) pulls a full export of that service in one pass — every
  report and every available document — into its folder.
- Some services expose a bulk **Documents** tab (select files, download) or, for large record sets, a
  one-shot **Export** (for example, a full health-record export).

## Removing data

- **Erase stored data** wipes one service's reports, keeping the session and any downloaded files.
- **Uninstall a service** removes its session, settings, and cached data, but keeps downloaded files —
  unless you tick the option to delete its whole data folder.
- **Clear all** (in Settings) wipes everything, after a two-click confirm.
- **Disconnect keeps your downloaded files** — it only drops the session.

There is no cloud, no sync, and no account. There's nowhere for your data to go — which is the entire point.
