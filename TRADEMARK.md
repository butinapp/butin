The code is open. The name is not.

## What you may do

- State compatibility factually — "a plugin for Butin", "works with Butin", "compatible with Butin".
- Use the name to refer to this project in articles, talks, comparisons, and documentation.
- Build Butin from source and keep the name on a build you compile for your own use.

## What you may not do

- Distribute a modified build under the name Butin. Fork freely; rename before you ship.
- Use the name, wordmark, logo, or a confusingly similar variation as the name of your product, service, company, application, package, or domain.
- Imply endorsement by, affiliation with, or official status from this project.
- Register "Butin" or a confusingly similar mark in any jurisdiction or namespace, including package registries and domain names.

## Why this policy exists

Butin captures your authenticated sessions and stores them encrypted on your own machine. Its entire value rests on being able to trust the build you are running.
A modified distribution carrying the same name would put that trust at risk for every user, not just the ones who installed it — which is the specific harm this
policy exists to prevent. Renaming your fork is what keeps that boundary legible.

Questions about permitted use: open an issue at <https://github.com/butinapp/butin>.
